# Brand — WhoAped

Watch-only wallet activity translated into a clear, live record.

_Current brand direction: The Live Ledger. See `DESIGN.md` for the full design system._

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F4F4EF` | Page background |
| Sheet | `#FFFFFF` | Content surface |
| Ledger ink | `#111313` | Primary text, dark sections |
| Quiet ink | `#5A605D` | Secondary text |
| Signal orange | `#F04B2F` | Primary action and sparse event markers |
| Positive green | `#137A52` | Received or active state |
| Route blue | `#4055D9` | Swap semantics and focus |

Orange is deliberately rare. Do not use gradients, neon, glass cards, or decorative crypto motifs.

## Typography

- Space Grotesk: headings, prominent controls, and brand wordmark.
- System sans-serif: longer body copy.
- IBM Plex Mono: exact amounts, addresses, and timestamps only.

Use short, tightly tracked headlines, a restrained 8 px spacing rhythm, and generous reading space.

## Voice

Lead with facts and actions. Name the wallet, action, assets, and protocol when useful. No hype, invented urgency, fake transaction data, or financial promises. Illustrative previews must be labeled as such.

## Brand mark

Use the Detected mark: open scan corners, a focused eye, and a single orange signal point. Preserve the geometry and colors. The source is `files/whoaped-app-icon.svg`.
