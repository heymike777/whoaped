The runtime tokens live in `apps/mobile/src/theme.ts`. Foreground/background, muted/background, and primary-ink/primary exceed WCAG AA contrast. Native blur surfaces always retain a visible border and opaque fallback.

## Typography — Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono

- Space Grotesk: display, headings, body, controls; linked as a native font asset
- IBM Plex Mono: amounts, addresses, timestamps, system labels; linked as a native font asset

Use tabular/monospaced figures for values that update. Headlines are short, sentence case, and tightly tracked.

## Tone and voice

Write the action first: “Swapped 100 SOL to 10,000 USDC.” Use exact assets and amounts. Keep provider context secondary.

Sound awake, not alarmist. WhoAped reports confirmed facts and never manufactures urgency.

Avoid crypto hype, exclamation marks, “alpha,” “revolutionary,” “seamless,” and unexplained protocol jargon.

## Usage

- Mint means live, active, or successful.
- Coral is reserved for buys, failures, and high-attention moments.
- Blue means swaps; gold means transfers; purple means NFTs.
- Use an 8 px spatial rhythm with 4 px adjustments.
- Keep touch targets at least 40 × 40 px and preserve system reduced-motion settings.
