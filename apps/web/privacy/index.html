Privacy Policy.

WhoAped is a watch-only tracker for public blockchain wallets. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what choices you have. Effective November 8, 2026.

We never ask for your private key or seed phrase. WhoAped observes public addresses; it cannot sign transactions or move your assets.

Who we are and what this covers

WhoAped is published by the Danger Valley team. This policy applies to the WhoAped website, iOS and Android apps, API, and Telegram bot. If you follow a link to another service—such as Solscan, a DEX, or a trading bot—that service has its own privacy practices.

Data we handle

  • Wallet tracking: the public addresses you add, wallet names you choose, chain and tracking preferences, public onchain transactions, parsed activity, and notification history. A wallet name or address may identify you or another person even though blockchain data is public.
  • Account and device: an app session identifier, device push token, platform, and the information needed to link an app account to your Telegram account when you choose to do so. The app stores its session token in device secure storage.
  • Telegram: your Telegram chat ID, username when provided by Telegram, commands, wallet changes, delivery status, and messages you send for support. Telegram itself processes your use of its service under its own policy.
  • Usage and diagnostics: app and bot interaction events, website visits if you accept website analytics, and technical data such as request IP address, browser or device information, error and security logs. Firebase Crashlytics also receives app crash reports, stack traces, device and app version information, and diagnostic identifiers to help us fix crashes. We do not intentionally attach wallet addresses, wallet names, or transaction hashes to mobile analytics or crash reports.
  • Payments: new Premium purchases are currently unavailable. If you have an earlier payment or support request, we may retain its payment reference, status, and correspondence to resolve it and meet applicable obligations.

Public blockchain records remain on the chain independently of WhoAped, even if you remove a wallet from our service.

How we use data

We use this information to create and secure your account; monitor addresses you choose; parse transactions; show activity, balances, and Discovery profiles; deliver push and Telegram alerts; prevent abuse and duplicate delivery; answer support requests; and understand whether the service works. Where applicable, these activities rely on providing the service you requested, our legitimate interests in security and improvement, consent for optional website analytics, or legal obligations for historical payments.

WhoAped does not use your private keys, execute trades for you, or make investment decisions for you.

Service providers and external links

We use DigitalOcean to host the API and database; Helius and other Solana data sources to retrieve and decode public chain data; Firebase/Google for push delivery, app analytics, and crash reporting; Google Analytics for consent-based website analytics and bot usage reporting; Vercel for the website; and Telegram to operate the bot. Providers process only the information needed for their role, subject to their own terms and privacy notices. Data may be processed in countries other than yours.

When you open an external token, DEX, explorer, social, or download link, the destination can receive information such as your IP address and the token or transaction in the URL. Some trading links may contain a WhoAped referral code. We do not control those sites or their data practices.

Analytics and website choices

The website does not load Google Analytics until you select Allow analytics. Your choice is stored in your browser. Use Privacy choices in the footer to change it; declining or withdrawing consent stops future website analytics on that browser. Mobile apps use Firebase Analytics for app lifecycle, screen-view, and wallet-add/remove events. The Telegram bot records usage events such as commands and wallet additions or removals to produce aggregate reports. These analytics are not a public list of your watched wallets.

How long we keep data

We keep account, wallet, and saved activity data while needed to provide the service and until deletion is requested or the data is no longer needed. Raw parsed ingestion events are scheduled to expire after seven days. Removing a wallet stops future tracking of that wallet, but does not by itself erase already recorded activity. Operational logs, analytics, support, and historical payment records may follow different retention periods where needed for security, troubleshooting, legal obligations, or disputes. Public onchain data cannot be removed from the blockchain by WhoAped.

Your choices and rights

You can remove tracked wallets, change failed-transaction preferences, disable push notifications in your device settings, and change website analytics consent using Privacy choices below. Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account before acting on it. To request deletion of your WhoAped account and associated stored data, use the contact method below; removing the app alone does not delete server-side data.

Security and children

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect stored data and account access, including encrypted connections and restricted access to production systems. No internet service is completely secure. WhoAped is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly invite children to create an account.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or a data request, open @whoapedbot on Telegram and send /paysupport Privacy request: [your message]. Do not include a seed phrase, private key, or password. We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will change when we publish a material revision.
