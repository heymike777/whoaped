# WhoAped sonar and radar directions — round 2

This set deliberately avoids literal wallets, eyes, and mascots. Every direction is built around detection, listening, range, and a single on-chain event.

1. **Radar Sweep** — recognizable radar with a quieter asymmetry.
2. **Echo Origin** — symmetrical sonar arcs around one detected event.
3. **Directional Ping** — a compact sweep with a clear origin and target.
4. **Sonar Beam** — layered echoes moving toward an event.
5. **Signal Core** — abstract concentric scanning rings.
6. **Dual Sonar** — listening from both sides with a subtle W-like base.
7. **Radar Wedge** — graphic scanning sector, the boldest option.
8. **Echo Drop** — a distinct sonar vessel shape rather than a generic circle.
9. **Radar W** — the most direct fusion of radar and the WhoAped initial.
10. **Ping Field** — a classic radar field with a highlighted event.
